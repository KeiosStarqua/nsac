HRP
Spacecraft Occupant Protection
SpaceX Crew Dragon Freedom spacecraft pictured as it lands in October 2022 with four crew members aboard. Re-entry to Earth is a central focus of ongoing NASA research because it’s a time of elevated injury risk and crew discomfort.
Credit: NASA
Description
This study will assess injury risk during landing by surveying crew members about any
discomfort they may experience during re-entry
to Earth and combining that information with sensor data from the spacecraft. This work specifically focuses on landing because it’s the most dynamic event of flight missions.
Impact
This information will influence future simulations and models to improve predictive injury work and help NASA design re-entry protocols and standards to protect crews as missions venture to the Moon, Mars, and beyond.

Flight
