# 4.8 Space Permissible Exposure Limit for Space Flight Radiation Exposure

The following standards define the Space Permissible Exposure Limits (PELs) for the following: astronaut total career exposure limits, short-term acute exposure limits and nuclear technology exposure limits. It is important to further minimize exposure from all sources of radiation below the following limits using the as low as reasonably achievable (ALARA) principle.
