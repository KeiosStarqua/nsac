# 5. Training (overview)

The following requirements outline the required training for personnel who support a space flight mission. Also refer to [V1 3005] Medical Care Training for In-Mission Medical Care Provider, and [V1 3006] Crew Medical Officers Quantity, for additional medical care training information.
