# 5.3 Crew Surgeon Training

[V1 5005] NASA and/or contractor FSs assigned to support the subject space program shall receive training and certification in accordance with a program-specific training plan. An example is JSC-26546, NASA International Space Station Flight Surgeon Training and Certification Plan.

[Rationale: For the subject program, this training includes courses such as mission controller certification (for all involved Mission Control Centers) advanced cardiac life support/advanced trauma life support (ACLS/ATLS), flight medicine procedures, aerospace physiology, space medicine, hyperbaric medicine, and emergency mishap response.]
