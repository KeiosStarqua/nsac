# 5.5 Support Personnel Training

[V1 5007] Supervised training programs shall be implemented for individuals who require knowledge of space medicine or flight medical procedures, such as flight directors, medical consultants, and/or other personnel deemed appropriate as part of the Medical and Crew Health Requirements Document.
