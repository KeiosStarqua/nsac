# 5.7 Physiological Exposure Mission Training

[V1 5009] Physiological training designed to assist crewmembers with pre-mission familiarization to in-flight exposures (i.e., carbon dioxide [CO2] exposure training, hypoxia training/instruction, centrifuge, and high-performance aircraft microgravity adaptation training) in preparation for space flight shall be provided.
